TITULO:
How does a tower catch Starship? #Starship

DESCRIPCION:
#Starship #SpaceX #Mechazilla

How does a launch tower catch a 52 m ship falling from space? No landing legs: two giant arms nicknamed the "chopsticks" wait for it. The ship does its belly flop toward the tower, three Raptor engines light and it swings upright in the landing flip, then it settles between the arms while the engines shut down one by one. On Flight 15 (Nov 14, 2026) the arms closed about 18 seconds after the engines lit (T+22:32:16 to T+22:32:34), after 14 orbits and ~22.5 hours in space. It has to reach exactly the right spot to be caught. Catching it means no landing legs and a faster inspection and preparation; quick turnaround has not been shown yet.
