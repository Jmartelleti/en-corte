TITULO:
How does a turbo make a small engine act like a big one? #Turbo

DESCRIPCION:
#Turbo #Cars #Engineering

How can a small engine make as much power as a big one? An engine makes power by burning fuel with air, so more air means more power. A turbocharger gets that air using exhaust gas the engine would otherwise waste: the hot exhaust spins a turbine, a shaft connects it to a compressor, and the compressor squeezes more air into the engine, which is called boost. The turbine needs exhaust to spin up first, which causes turbo lag, and a wastegate lets extra exhaust bypass the turbine so boost never gets too high.
