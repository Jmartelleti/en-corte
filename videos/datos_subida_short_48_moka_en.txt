TITULO:
How does a coffee pot push water uphill using only a flame? #MokaPot

DESCRIPCION:
#MokaPot #Coffee #Engineering

A moka pot has three parts: a boiler with water, a funnel holding the coffee grounds, and a top chamber. On the stove, the air and steam trapped in the sealed boiler build pressure. That pressure can only push down on the water, so the water climbs up the funnel, soaks through the grounds, and the coffee rises up the center tube into the top chamber. A rubber seal keeps the pressure in, and a safety valve lets steam out if it gets too high. A moka pot works at roughly 1 to 2 bar, while an espresso machine uses about 9 bar, which is why moka coffee is not true espresso.
