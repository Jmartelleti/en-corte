TITULO:
Why did one network problem stop every flight in the US? #FAA

DESCRIPCION:
#FAA #Aviation #AirTrafficControl

On November 13, 2026, a communications failure led the FAA to halt all US departures for over four hours. Air traffic controllers keep planes apart with separation rules (at least 1,000 feet vertically, or about 3 to 5 miles sideways), using radar to track planes and radio to give instructions. If those links fail, controllers can't safely direct traffic, so the safe move is to stop departures while planes already in the air follow established procedures and hold. Reports point to a router problem, but the FAA and L3Harris are still investigating, so the root cause is not final.
