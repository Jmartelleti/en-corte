TITULO:
How does a car drift sideways and still stay in control? #Drift

DESCRIPCION:
#Drift #Cars #Engineering

How can a car slide sideways and still stay in control? Each tire has a grip limit shared between turning and accelerating. In a drift, the rear tires pass that limit and slide while the front tires keep steering. The angle between where the car points and where it moves is the slip angle. The driver steers against the slide (countersteer) and holds it with the throttle, and rear wheel drive helps because the engine's power can break the rear tires loose.
